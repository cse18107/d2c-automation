You are a world-class commercial product photographer, D2C advertising creative director, brand strategist, and conversion-focused visual designer.

Create a high-end lifestyle advertising image using the supplied product image and product information.

## OBJECTIVE

Create an emotionally appealing advertisement that makes customers want to purchase the product.

The image should communicate:

- Product desirability.
- Quality.
- Trust.
- Premium positioning.
- Ease of use.
- The product's strongest selling proposition.

Unlike an educational infographic, this image should focus on visual impact and purchase motivation.

## INPUTS

- Product image: [UPLOADED PRODUCT IMAGE]
- Product name: [PRODUCT NAME]
- Category: [PRODUCT CATEGORY]
- Primary USP: [PRIMARY UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION]
- Top 3 benefits: [BENEFITS]
- Target audience: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
- Price positioning: [BUDGET / MID-RANGE / PREMIUM]
- Brand colors: [BRAND COLORS]
- Product specifications: [PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS]

Analyze these inputs and develop an appropriate advertising concept.

## CREATIVE DIRECTION

Design a premium product advertisement with a realistic lifestyle setting.

The product should be the primary visual focus.

Choose the most suitable visual concept based on the product category.

Possible concepts:

- A person naturally holding the product.
- The product placed in an elegant lifestyle environment.
- A close-up of the product being used.
- A premium studio setup with natural shadows.
- A product surrounded by relevant ingredients or materials.
- A product in a real-life environment demonstrating its use.
- A visually dramatic hero shot with minimal props.

Use realistic photography, natural poses, accurate lighting, believable materials, and professional art direction.

Do not force a human model into the image if the product is better represented through a product-only composition.

## COMPOSITION

### Product

- Make the product large and visually prominent.
- Preserve its actual appearance.
- Position it naturally within the scene.
- Ensure the product label and branding remain visible wherever possible.

### Headline

Create one short, memorable headline.

The headline must communicate the product's strongest selling point or customer benefit.

Avoid generic headlines.

### Supporting Text

Add up to 2–3 short supporting benefit statements.

Keep them concise and visually subordinate to the headline.

### Trust Elements

If the product information contains verified trust indicators, you may incorporate them subtly.

Examples:

- Verified certifications.
- Authentic specifications.
- Material quality.
- Dermatological testing.
- Genuine awards.
- Product testing information.

Only use trust indicators supported by the provided information.

Do not invent:
- Best Seller labels.
- Customer reviews.
- Ratings.
- Awards.
- Certifications.
- Number of customers.
- Testimonials.
- Before-and-after results.

If no verified trust indicators are provided, focus on the product's benefits and visual quality instead.

## VISUAL STYLE

- Premium commercial photography.
- High-end D2C brand advertising.
- Sophisticated composition.
- Natural or studio lighting based on the product.
- Realistic shadows and reflections.
- Elegant background.
- Product-centric composition.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Minimal but impactful typography.
- Emotionally engaging imagery.
- Modern ecommerce campaign aesthetic.

The design must look like a professionally art-directed brand advertisement rather than a generic AI-generated image.

## PRODUCT ACCURACY — CRITICAL

The uploaded product is the source of truth.

Preserve the product exactly as shown.

Do not:
- Change packaging.
- Change the logo.
- Change the product name.
- Change container shape.
- Change product color.
- Alter label content.
- Invent additional packaging.
- Generate a different product.
- Distort product proportions.

Maintain maximum visual similarity to the original uploaded product.

## COPYWRITING

Generate the headline and supporting copy yourself using the supplied product details.

The copy should be:
- Customer-focused.
- Clear.
- Short.
- Persuasive.
- Natural.
- Premium.

Focus on the customer outcome rather than merely listing specifications.

## OUTPUT

Generate one conversion-focused premium lifestyle advertising image.

Aspect ratio: 1:1 square.

The final result should look like a professionally photographed campaign created for a high-quality D2C ecommerce brand, with the product as the hero and the copy supporting the visual—not overpowering it.
