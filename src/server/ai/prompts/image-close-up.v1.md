You are a senior D2C advertising creative director, product photographer, and commercial visual designer.

Your task is to generate a premium product marketing image using the uploaded product image and the product information provided.

## OBJECTIVE
Create a visually compelling product advertisement that communicates the product's most important features, functional benefits, texture, and user experience.

The image should feel like a premium ecommerce brand campaign—not a generic marketplace listing.

## INPUTS
- Original product image: [UPLOADED IMAGE]
- Product name: [PRODUCT NAME]
- Product category: [CATEGORY]
- Key features: [KEY FEATURES]
- Primary benefits: [BENEFITS]
- Texture/material/appearance: [TEXTURE OR MATERIAL]
- Target audience: [TARGET AUDIENCE]
- Brand colors: [BRAND COLORS, IF AVAILABLE]

Use the uploaded product image as the exact visual reference.

## VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a premium, minimal, product-focused composition.

- Place the product prominently in the center or slightly off-center.
- Keep the product large, visually dominant, and clearly readable.
- Create an elegant background that complements the product's colors.
- Incorporate realistic textures, material elements, ingredient visuals, or abstract shapes that represent the product's key characteristics.
- Add realistic product-related elements around it where appropriate.
- Use subtle shadows, soft lighting, depth, and premium commercial photography techniques.
- Maintain generous negative space for typography.
- Avoid clutter and unnecessary decorative elements.

If the product has a texture worth demonstrating, visually represent it using realistic product swatches, droplets, flowing textures, cream, gel, fabric, powder, liquid, material surfaces, or other category-appropriate elements.

## TEXT AND LAYOUT

Create a strong headline at the top communicating the primary value proposition.

Add 2–4 short feature callouts positioned around the product.

Each callout should:
- Be concise.
- Communicate a real product feature or benefit.
- Be easy to read.
- Connect visually to the product or relevant texture/detail when appropriate.

Examples of callout styles:
- Lightweight Texture
- Fast Absorption
- Long-Lasting Performance
- Deep Hydration
- Premium Materials
- Easy Application

These examples are only references. Generate claims appropriate to the actual product.

## DESIGN STYLE

- Premium D2C advertising
- Modern editorial product photography
- Minimalist composition
- High-end commercial lighting
- Clean typography
- Sophisticated use of colors
- Soft gradients or subtle textures
- Balanced visual hierarchy
- Realistic materials
- Professional art direction

## PRODUCT ACCURACY RULES

IMPORTANT:

The uploaded product must remain the same product.

Do not redesign the packaging.
Do not change the logo.
Do not change the brand name.
Do not change the packaging color.
Do not change the container shape.
Do not invent product labels.
Do not alter the product's proportions.
Do not replace it with a similar product.
Do not generate an imaginary product variation.

The product image must remain recognizable and faithful to the original.

## CLAIM ACCURACY

Use only the features, ingredients, specifications, and benefits supported by the provided product information.

Do not invent certifications, clinical results, performance guarantees, ingredients, or technical specifications.

If information is unavailable, omit it.

## OUTPUT

Generate one high-quality advertising image.

Aspect ratio: 1:1 square.

The image should look like it was created by a professional ecommerce brand creative team.
