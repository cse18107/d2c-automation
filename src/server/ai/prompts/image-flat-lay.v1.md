## OBJECTIVE

Create an educational yet visually attractive advertising creative that quickly explains:

1. What the product does.
2. Its most important benefits.
3. Why the customer should choose it.
4. How the product improves the customer's experience or solves a problem.

The design should communicate the product's value within a few seconds of viewing.

## COMPOSITION

Create a modern ecommerce infographic with two distinct visual zones.

### Zone 1 — Headline

Place a large, bold headline at the top.

The headline should communicate the product's main value proposition in simple language.

Examples of headline structures:

- "2-in-1: Performance + Comfort"
- "Designed for Everyday Protection"
- "Powerful Results, Simplified"
- "Everything You Need in One Product"

Do not copy these examples blindly. Write a headline specifically for the supplied product.

### Zone 2 — Product Benefits

Create 2–3 benefit sections.

Each section should contain:

- A small visual icon, illustration, or relevant photographic element.
- A short benefit heading.
- A brief explanation of the benefit, ideally no longer than one sentence.

Benefits should be clearly differentiated and easy to scan.

### Zone 3 — Lifestyle or Human Visual

Incorporate a lifestyle photograph, human interaction, close-up detail, or realistic visual demonstrating the product's purpose.

Choose the visual based on the product category.

Examples:

- Skincare → skin, application, hydration, beauty routine.
- Food → serving, eating, ingredients, freshness.
- Electronics → product in use, interaction, functionality.
- Fitness → movement, performance, active lifestyle.
- Home products → actual usage in a home environment.
- Fashion → person wearing or styling the product.
- Accessories → real-world usage.

The lifestyle visual must reinforce the product's actual benefits.

Use the uploaded product image as the product reference.

Preserve:
- Product packaging.
- Product silhouette.
- Brand logo.
- Product name.
- Colors.
- Materials.
- Label details.
- Overall appearance.

Do not redesign, distort, or invent the product.

The product must remain recognizable and visually accurate.

## INFORMATION ACCURACY

All benefit claims must be grounded in the provided product details.

Do not invent:
- Clinical claims.
- Medical claims.
- Performance guarantees.
- Ingredients.
- Certifications.
- Product capabilities.
- Statistics.

Translate technical information into easy-to-understand customer language without changing its meaning.
